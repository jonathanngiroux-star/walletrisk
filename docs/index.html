How it works

01 Discovery

Your holdings and contract interactions are read from public Blockscout instances — keyless, no tracking, nothing stored.

02 Live checks

Each candidate pair (token × spender) is verified with a live allowance() call via publicnode RPC. Only real, current approvals are reported.

03 Risk model

UNLIMITED approvals on tokens you hold are HIGH severity. Dormant unlimited approvals are MEDIUM. Small allowances are LOW.

04 You revoke

Every finding links to revoke.cash, where you revoke with your own wallet. WalletRisk never asks for keys and never signs anything.
